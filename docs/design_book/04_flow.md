# 04 — Flow: Spawns, Movement, Combat Rhythm

## Spawns

- No spawn may be visible from another team's spawn (audit sightlines!).
- A freshly spawned player needs: 2+ exit directions, immediate cover within
  300 cm, and a visible landmark to orient by.
- Never spawn facing a wall. Face the action or the landmark.
- Keep spawn zones out of grenade/projectile reach of contestable space.

## Loops beat dead ends

Connect spaces in circuits: a player should (almost) always have 2 ways out.
Chase sequences, flanking, and "I'm being followed" tension all die in
cul-de-sacs. Dead ends are legitimate ONLY as: reward rooms (loot at the
end), scare set-pieces (horror), or sniper perches with a risk trade-off.
Heuristic: ≥70% of rooms/junctions have ≥2 exits.

## Cover rhythm

In any space where combat is expected, provide cover every 500–1000 cm along
movement lines. Cover = ≥110 cm tall (crouch) or ≥192 (stand). Plazas without
cover are kill boxes — intentional only when one side is MEANT to be exposed.

## Sightline audit

Walk the three longest sightlines of the map (use viewport at player height).
For each: who holds it, from where, and what's the counterplay (flank route,
cover approach, smoke)? A sightline без counterplay is a balance bug.

## Verticality

High ground = power. Every strong position needs ≥2 approaches and an
exposure cost (silhouette against sky, audible route). Drop-downs are
one-way valves — great for pacing, but check the player can't soft-lock
into a pit (always an exit or death plane).

## Pace structure (PvE / experience maps)

Alternate intensity: tension → release → tension. Spatially: tight → open →
tight. A map that is uniformly tight (or open) numbs the player. Sketch the
intended intensity curve before building zones; the labyrinth's openings and
chambers ARE the pacing tool.

## Distance budgets

Walking is content only when something changes en route. >15 s of identical
corridor (≈ 9 000 cm at run speed) without an event/visual change = cut it
or add a beat.
